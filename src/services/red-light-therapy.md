Red light therapy is a non-invasive treatment that uses low-level red and near-infrared light wavelengths to penetrate the skin and promote cellular healing. The therapy stimulates the body's natural recovery processes at a cellular level.

### How It Works

During a session, targeted light wavelengths are delivered to the skin and underlying tissue. These wavelengths stimulate mitochondrial activity, increasing the production of ATP (cellular energy). This boost in cellular energy accelerates the body's natural healing and regeneration processes.

### Available Sessions

**Face Red Light Therapy** — a focused 15-minute session targeting the face to promote collagen production, reduce fine lines, and improve skin tone and texture.

**Full Body Red Light Therapy** — a 30-minute session treating the entire body to help with muscle recovery, reduce inflammation, ease joint pain, and support overall wellness.

Red light therapy can be used as a standalone treatment or as a complement to massage therapy for enhanced recovery benefits.
